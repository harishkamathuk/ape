# Decision Principles to IPM Mapping

## Purpose

This document maps decision questions in the Decision Principles Catalogue to the corresponding IPM sections. It is policy-only and does not prescribe implementation.

## Changelog

- 0.1.0 (2026-02-07): Initial mapping.

## Mapping

| Decision Question ID | IPM Section | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| DPQ-001 | IPM: Authority and Change Control | Authority context governs whether actions are permitted. |
| DPQ-002 | IPM: Rebalancing Philosophy | Drift and bands define when rebalancing is required. |
| DPQ-003 | IPM: Strategic Asset Allocation (SAA) | Contributions affect how target weights are restored. |
| DPQ-004 | IPM: Risk Framework | Risk limits and drawdown thresholds govern action. |
